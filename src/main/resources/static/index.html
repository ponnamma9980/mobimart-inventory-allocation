Recommendation Logic

How the inventory allocation engine makes decisions

MobiMart analyzes historical sales data to estimate average weekly demand for each phone at each store. The system targets approximately two weeks of inventory coverage and compares this target with current inventory. Products with inventory gaps are evaluated using store attractiveness, stockout impact, product lifecycle and demand. The final allocation is constrained by the ₹4 crore chain-wide inventory budget.
